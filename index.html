What we do

You get what suits your house — not what happens to be in stock.

A little too tall, a little too wide, an arch nobody else will quote on. That is the work we enjoy most.

Every VYNA window and door is drawn for one single opening. You choose the colour, the glazing bars, the hardware, the glass and the way it opens. If you cannot find what you are looking for, we draw it for you — and we keep drawing until it is right. Our base is on the Costa del Sol and we deliver throughout Spain.

Grants

Spain will help pay for it.

Replacing old windows is one of the most effective things you can do for a home's energy performance — and the Spanish state rewards it.

Homeowners can claim back up to 40 % of the cost through the national income tax deduction, and larger renovation projects can reach considerably more. We prepare the documentation your technician needs, and we tell you what to do before the work starts — which is the part most people get wrong.

We come and measure

We visit the property, measure every opening ourselves and look at what the building is asking for.

You get drawings and a fixed price. We change them as many times as it takes. Nothing goes into production until you are happy.

Made to order in solid timber, then finished, glazed and packed as one delivery.

Delivered to site anywhere in Spain. On the Costa del Sol we fit it ourselves; elsewhere we make sure your installers have everything they need to do it properly.
