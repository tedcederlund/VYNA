Partners and resellers

Put VYNA in front of your customers.

Kitchen studios, interior designers, building merchants, window fitters — if your customers ask for windows and doors that are a cut above, we would like to talk. We make it, you sell it, and your customer ends up with something they will be proud of for decades.

Why VYNA

A product that sells itself the moment people touch it.

Solid sapele, energy glass and Scandinavian workmanship, made to the millimetre for each opening. It is the kind of product customers understand from the first look — and the kind that brings them back to you for the next project.

VYNA is the window and door brand of Curtex Exterior, part of Curtex Group. Behind every partner there is a company with its own office, its own team and a long-term view. We are not looking for volume at any price; we are looking for partners we can grow with.

Help with every quote

A team that knows the product

Material to sell with

Delivery taken care of
