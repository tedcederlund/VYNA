Materials

The wood that gets better the longer you own it.

We build in sapele and sipo — two close relatives of mahogany that behave beautifully in a Mediterranean climate and look better every year they spend in the sun.

Colour

A warmth you cannot paint on.

Sapele starts a golden reddish brown and deepens, over the years, into something closer to old mahogany. The grain runs in alternating directions, which is what produces that soft ribbon shimmer across a sash when the light moves round the house.

It is the reason people who see it oiled rarely want it painted. But if you do want colour, it takes a finish beautifully — any RAL or NCS shade, matt or satin, inside and out, and the two do not have to match.

Climate

Made for heat, sun and salt air.

Spain asks a lot of joinery. On the coast it is the sun, the humidity and the salt on the wind. Inland it is the swing between a forty-degree afternoon and a cold night, which is what moves timber and opens joints. Sapele takes both in its stride.

It is dense and steady, so a tall door leaf stays flat and a window keeps closing sweetly through the seasons. It has good natural resistance to damp, rot and insects. And because the trees grow tall and straight, we can cut long clean lengths — which means big sliding panels and full-height doors without a joint where you would rather not see one.

The hardware is chosen for the same reason. Fittings that shrug off salt, on a wood that shrugs off sun.

Longevity

Maintainable, not maintenance-free.

We are careful about that distinction. Maintenance-free usually means unrepairable — when something fails, the whole unit goes in a skip. Our products are made to be looked after and, if they are ever damaged, repaired.

In practice that means an oil or a light refresh every few years on the exposed side, and very little else. If you would rather not think about it at all, we clad the weather face in powder-coated aluminium and leave the timber on the inside where you can see and touch it.

A window looked after this way outlives the person who chose it. That is rather the point.

Responsibility

We know where our timber comes from.

Tropical hardwood carries an obligation and we take it seriously. We work with traceable material and documented supply chains, and we would rather turn down an order than lose track of where the wood was cut.

The same goes for what we put on it. Water-based paints and environmentally certified sealants, chosen to be kind to the people applying them as well as to the house they end up on.

If you want the paperwork behind any of this, ask. We would be glad to show you.

Come and hold a sample.

Wood is hard to judge from a screen. We keep samples at the office in San Pedro Alcántara and you are welcome to come and see the difference between oiled and painted in daylight. If you are further away, we will post them to you.

Energy grants in Spain

Up to 40 % of your window replacement, back in your pocket.

Changing old windows is one of the most effective energy improvements you can make to a home in Spain — and there is public money to encourage it. Most owners simply never claim it.

What is available

Two routes, and they can be combined.

The national income tax deduction gives private owners a percentage of the cost back through their Spanish tax return, rising with the energy improvement achieved. For a typical window replacement that is up to 40 %.

On top of that, every autonomous community runs its own grant programmes for energy renovation, Andalucía included. Where a whole building is upgraded and the savings are substantial, the combined support can go considerably higher.

Which one fits your project depends on the property, the scope of work and what is open at the time. We will tell you plainly which is worth pursuing — including when the honest answer is neither.

Get this part right

Three things decide whether you get the money.

Apply before the work starts

This is where most claims are lost. Begin the installation before the application is in and the grant is usually gone for good, with no way to correct it. Talk to us early.

Get the energy certificate done twice

A qualified technician certifies the property before the work and again afterwards, showing the improvement achieved. We can point you to people who do this.

Pay traceably

Bank transfer or card, to a registered company, with a proper invoice. Cash payments cannot be claimed.

Our part

We give your technician everything they need.

We are window makers, not tax advisers — but we know which documents an application asks for, and we prepare them without being chased.

Performance figures for the products you have chosen, declarations of performance, an invoice specified the way the authorities want to see it, and a clear before-and-after description of what was replaced. If you need a technician or a gestor, we will point you toward one we have worked with.

About us

A company built by friends who like getting it right.

VYNA brings Scandinavian window making to Spain — the deep frames, the precise seals and the stubborn insistence on quality — and adapts all of it to a climate of sun, heat and salt.

Why we exist

Because a window is not just a window.

It is the light on a Sunday morning, the quiet when the road outside is busy, and the warmth of a room at the end of the day. It is also the only part of a house you look at from both sides.

Too many homes on this coast were fitted with whatever was in stock, in a size that was near enough. We think you should get the opposite: something drawn for your house, in a material that ages well, made once and made properly.

High, low, round or entirely one of a kind — we build what fits you, not the other way round.

What you can hold us to

Four promises.

We respect the building

We adapt to the house rather than asking the house to adapt to us. That matters as much in a century-old finca as in a new architect-designed villa.

We build things that last

Solid materials, energy glass and timeless proportions. Not a cost but an investment in the house — and a great deal kinder to the planet than replacing something every fifteen years.

We make it easy

Thoughtful details, low maintenance and one person who knows your project from the first visit to the last adjustment.

We are decent to deal with

Honesty, listening and solving problems together. It is why our customers come back and why our partnerships last.
